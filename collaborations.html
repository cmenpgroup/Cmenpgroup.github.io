Collaborations

Canisius Medium Energy Nuclear Physics Group

Dr. Michael H. Wood (often referred to in contexts as Michel Wood), a Professor of Physics at Canisius University, conducts cutting-edge experimental nuclear physics research at Jefferson Lab (Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility) in Newport News, Virginia. His work focuses on the dynamics of the subatomic nucleus—particularly quark hadronization and meson absorption in nuclear media—as well as searches for Dark Matter particles using electron beams. Through collaborations with large international experiments like CLAS at Jefferson Lab and the Beam Dump eXperiment (BDX), Dr. Wood advances fundamental understanding of particle physics while actively involving undergraduate students in hands-on research at one of the world’s premier electron accelerators.

Research Collaborators and Partners

  • CLAS Collaboration at Jefferson Lab (various physicists contributing to meson and hadron studies).
  • Beam Dump eXperiment (BDX) Collaboration — International team exploring Dark Matter production and secondary particle beams using Jefferson Lab’s beam dump.
  • Physicists and graduate students from Istituto Nazionale di Fisica Nucleare (INFN), Genova, Italy.
  • Researchers from Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María, Valparaíso, Chile.
  • Undergraduate students from Canisius University — Regularly involved in experiments and data analysis at Jefferson Lab.
